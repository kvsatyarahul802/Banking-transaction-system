VaultView is a Banking Transaction Management and Analysis System designed to manage accounts, record transactions, and understand financial activity through reports and charts.

Our Features

Account Management

View and manage customer accounts and account details.

Deposit Management

Record deposits and maintain transaction information.

Withdrawal Management

Record withdrawals and validate transaction amounts.

Fund Transfers

Transfer funds between accounts in the simulation.

Transaction History

View previous transactions and their details.

Data Analysis

Analyze deposits, withdrawals, and account activity.

Monthly Reports

Understand monthly transaction totals and trends.

Transaction Monitoring

Flag unusual transactions for review using predefined rules.
